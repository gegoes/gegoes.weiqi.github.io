A few useful things

Liberties, capture & suicide

A stone's liberties are the empty intersections beside it. Stones joined along the grid share liberties. Remove an opposing group when its last liberty is filled. A move that leaves your own group without liberties is forbidden, unless that move captures first.

Ko, passing & ending

You cannot recreate an earlier board position. Pass when you have no useful moves; two consecutive passes end the game and begin scoring.

How to read the score

This board estimates Chinese area scoring: each living stone and each empty point surrounded by only one color counts as one point. White receives komi for going second. Disputed open space is neutral.
