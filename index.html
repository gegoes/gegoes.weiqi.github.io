    Basics

    Liberties & captures

    Liberties are the empty intersections adjacent to a stone. When you surround a group completely, those stones are captured.

    Ko & passing

    You cannot recreate an earlier position (ko rule). Pass when you have no moves; two passes end the game and score.

    Scoring

    Chinese area scoring counts stones plus surrounded empty territory.
